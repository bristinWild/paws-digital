# Playing PAWS — Wild Ventures

Open the link your host gave you. You'll land on a welcome screen with
Naledi (your guide) and four ways to play.

Before picking a mode, type your name in **Your name** and choose a
**Game length** — both apply to whichever mode you start next.

## Learn to play

Pick **Learn to play**. Naledi walks you through the game one step at a
time — one rule, one action, one lesson at a time — against a fixed,
friendly deal. This is the best first game for a brand-new player, solo,
at your own pace.

## Solo game

Pick **Solo game** to race Naledi to the Rain Dance on your own, no one
else needed. Naledi plays her own seat; you play yours.

## Play with bots

Pick **Play with bots** to fill a table without needing other people online
at the same time:

1. Choose a **Table size** (how many seats total).
2. Start the game — any seat you didn't fill yourself is taken by a bot that
   plays its own turns automatically, at a human pace, so you can watch it
   decide rather than have the whole table resolve instantly.

You can mix this with real friends too: open a **Play with friends** room
(below), have as many humans join as you like, and start it as a "bots"
table size instead if you want the rest filled automatically — ask your host
which room type they created if you're not sure.

## Play with friends (room codes)

**To host:**

1. Pick **Play with friends**.
2. Once the room screen opens, share the room code (or copy the invite
   link, which already points at the right address) with your friends.
3. Wait for at least one other person to join — you'll see each name appear
   as they do.
4. When everyone's in, press **Start the game**.

**To join:**

1. Open the **same link** your host sent you (same server, same address).
2. Type your name.
3. Type the 6-character **room code** into the box next to **Join** on the
   welcome screen, and press **Join**.
4. Wait for the host to start the game — you'll see the table appear
   automatically once they do.

You don't need to keep a tab open and waiting the whole time: if you close
your browser or lose connection mid-game, reopening the same link on the
same device picks your seat back up automatically (look for **Back to my
saved seat** on the welcome screen). If someone else needs to take over your
seat while you're away, the host can do that after you've been disconnected
for about 10 seconds — reconnecting afterward hands your seat back to you.

## Playing your turn

Whoever's turn it is sees their available actions highlighted on the table.
Tap/click a die, then the action space it can afford, and follow the
prompts (which cards to place, which animals to feed, and so on). The game
won't let you make an illegal move — if something looks disabled, it isn't
currently legal for that die.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Room not found"** — double-check the code (it's exactly 6 characters)
  and make sure you're using the same link/address your host sent, not an
  old one.
- **"Your seat could not be restored"** — you joined this room before but on
  a different device/browser (or cleared your browser data); ask the host
  for the room code and join again as a new seat if there's still room.
- **Game looks frozen** — check your connection; the table updates live as
  soon as you're reconnected, no refresh needed. A manual refresh is always
  safe and will put you right back where you left off.
